How the System Works
AgriChain transforms verified agricultural activities into a trusted financial identity, enabling farmers to access financial services based on their real farming performance.

HOW TO OPERATE(Detailed)

Step 1: Create an Account or just choose one of the test accounts(WE VALUE YOUR TIME)
The farmer registers for an AgriChain account and completes their personal profile.
↓
Step 2: Register Farms
The farmer registers one or more farms, including details such as location, size, and crops grown.
↓
Step 3: Record Farming Activities
The farmer records verified farming activities such as land preparation, planting, fertilizer application, irrigation, pest control, and other farm operations.
↓
Step 4: Record Harvests
After harvesting, the farmer records the quantity and type of produce harvested from each farm.
↓
Step 5: Sell Produce Through the Marketplace
The harvested produce is listed on the AgriChain marketplace, where verified buyers can purchase it. Every completed transaction is securely recorded on the platform.
↓
Step 6: Build a Verified Farming History
The system continuously collects and verifies the farmer's agricultural and financial activities, including:
Farming activities
Harvest records
Produce sales
Transaction history
Buyer interactions
Farm productivity
Previous loan repayments (if any)
↓
Step 7: Generate a Lending Score
Using the verified records, AgriChain calculates a Lending Score that represents the farmer's reliability and financial potential.
The score is based on factors such as:
Number of successful produce sales
Sales consistency
Revenue generated
Farm productivity
Verified farming activities
Loan repayment history
Cooperative or agricultural officer verification
↓
Step 8: Apply for a Loan
Once the farmer has built a Lending Score, they can browse available loan products and submit a loan application through the platform.
↓
Step 9: Financial Institution Reviews the Application
The financial institution reviews the farmer's:
Lending Score
Farming history
Verified sales records
Harvest history
Financial performance
Previous repayment history
This enables lenders to make informed lending decisions using verified agricultural data rather than relying solely on traditional collateral.
↓
Step 10: Loan Approval or Rejection
Based on the assessment, the financial institution either approves or rejects the loan application.
↓
Step 11: Loan Disbursement
If approved, the loan is disbursed to the farmer, who can use the funds to purchase farming inputs, expand production, or invest in their farming activities.
↓
Step 12: Loan Repayment
The farmer repays the loan according to the agreed repayment schedule. Every repayment is securely recorded within AgriChain.
↓
Step 13: Strengthen Financial Identity
Successful loan repayments and continued marketplace activity improve the farmer's Lending Score over time, increasing their eligibility for larger loans, better financing terms, and additional financial opportunities.

AgriChain Lending Score
The AgriChain Lending Score is a data-driven score that measures a farmer's financial trustworthiness based on verified agricultural and marketplace activities.
The score considers factors such as:
Verified produce sales
Sales frequency and consistency
Total revenue generated
Harvest performance
Farm productivity
Farming activity records
Loan repayment performance
Cooperative verification
Agricultural officer verification
Transaction reliability
A higher Lending Score demonstrates a stronger financial profile, improving the farmer's chances of accessing affordable loans and other financial services.

AgriChain Value Proposition
Farmer → Farm Registration → Farming Activities → Harvest → Marketplace Sales → Verified Transaction History → Lending Score → Loan Application → Financial Institution Review → Loan Approval → Farm Growth → Loan Repayment → Higher Lending Score → Better Future Credit Access
AgriChain is more than a farming application—it is a digital financial infrastructure that transforms real agricultural activity into a trusted financial identity, empowering smallholder farmers with greater access to credit, markets, and economic opportunities.
